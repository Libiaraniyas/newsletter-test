Arabica Coffee Hits 10-Week Low On Brazil Weather And Exports
Arabica coffee prices have declined to a 10-week low due to favorable Brazilian weather conditions and increased export activity, easing cost pressures on coffee-dependent manufacturers and retailers. This commodity price correction presents both margin recovery opportunities for downstream players and potential margin compression risks for upstream producers.
